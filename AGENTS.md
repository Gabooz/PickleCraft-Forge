# PickleCraft development

For every addition, include all new player-facing items and blocks in the PickleCraft creative tab. Add a spawn egg for each new mob and include it in the same tab.

Update the Creative Zone Map for each addition. Show locations for world features and zone content. For additions without a world location, update the relevant controls or reference information on the map.

Verify the creative tab, spawn eggs, and map before considering the work complete.

## Item textures

Follow [the eight-layer item texture method](docs/item-texture-method.md).
Record aesthetic intent separately from structural requirements. After the
neutral draft, redesign the expressive item from a blank canvas or scene.
Review every finished item against vanilla at inventory size. Reject any
unexplained departure and send concrete notes to the structural requirements
step. Repeat through the comparison until the item passes.
