# PickleCraft development

For every addition, include all new player-facing items and blocks in the PickleCraft creative tab. Add a spawn egg for each new mob and include it in the same tab.

Update the Creative Zone Map for each addition. Show locations for world features and zone content. For additions without a world location, update the relevant controls or reference information on the map.

Verify the creative tab, spawn eggs, and map before considering the work complete.

## Item textures

Use the approved [eleven-layer texture method](docs/item-texture-method.md)
for every new or revised PickleCraft item texture. Use the documented
derived-texture procedure for the two flagged saw parts.
Record aesthetic intent separately, then make a fresh expressive design.
Use cleanup as the second half of the expressive redesign. Explore boldly,
then redraw shapes, joins, and shading wherever needed for a readable
result. Finish with a fresh vanilla comparison and local pixel cleanup,
then recheck any edited art.
Require both the strict vanilla comparison and an independent audit of the
finished art against the Layer 4 reconstruction recipe and Layer 6
construction rules. Write the Layer 4 recipe from the ground up and replay it
from a blank canvas or scene after each revision until it reconstructs the
intended item without guesses.
Save that recipe before the drafts. A replay needs a fresh reconstruction
from the written recipe. Exporting and rereading the finished art does not
count. Record exact vanilla references and match criteria. A screenshot
alone cannot pass the vanilla review. Missing review evidence leaves the
item pending until the missing layer and its successors are done.
Either failure returns the item to Layer 4 with concrete notes. Repeat both
reviews on every revision and ship only when both pass on that revision.
Finish the 2D or 3D decision by checking for a derived-texture flag. Only
the saw handle and saw blade have this flag. Derive both from the approved
assembled iron saw and use the four-step parent comparison for those two.
